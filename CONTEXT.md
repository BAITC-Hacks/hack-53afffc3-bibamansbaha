# EKT purchasing assistance

## Language

**Requested line**: A customer's requested item and quantity, retaining its source document position.

**Catalog product**: An item identified by a source ID and a separate article/SKU. Availability and price are facts with provenance.

**Alternative**: A candidate meeting all known critical requirements, with differences and unknowns disclosed. It is not a certified replacement.

**Proposal**: A versioned preview of selected products, quantities, prices and exclusions awaiting explicit customer confirmation.

**Confirmation**: A direct customer action approving one particular proposal version.

**Prototype cart**: The session's saved selection after confirmation; it is neither a stock reservation nor an order on ekt.kz.
